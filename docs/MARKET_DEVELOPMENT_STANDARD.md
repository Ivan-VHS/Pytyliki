# Market development

The machine-readable `market-development.v1.json` is the source of truth.

| Gate | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Audience | observed user job and friction |
| Position | shipped behavior + reproducible proof |
| Offer | platforms, prerequisites, limits, license |
| Promotion | relevant channels and factual claims |
| Feedback | recorded issues and user workflows |
| Commercialization | rights, demand, costs, support, pricing review |

No market-fit, revenue or profitability claim is inferred from a plan or profile.
